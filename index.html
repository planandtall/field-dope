Wind rule. Wind from 3 o'clock (your right) pushes the bullet left: dial RIGHT the clicks shown, or hold that far into the wind. From 9 o'clock, dial LEFT. From 2, 4, 8 or 10 use about ⅞ (7 of every 8 clicks, ~0.87); from 1, 5, 7 or 11 use half; 12 or 6, none. The table is 10 mph: 5 mph is half, 20 mph double.

Clicks are UP from your zero, ¼ MOA each. Turret is the number the elevation turret should read. Cold is the same shot at 25°F, for the first hour of a frosty morning. Wind clicks are for 10 mph full value. Hold is where the vitals go below center if you don't dial.

The sequence
  1. Range it. Rangefinder first, landmark card second, reticle last.
  2. Inside the dead-on window? Crosshair on the front-leg crease and shoot. Don't touch the turret.
  3. Past it: dial UP to the turret number shown. Read the number rather than counting clicks.
  4. Wind: hold into it. The vitals sit on the downwind side of the reticle; the crosshair ends up upwind.
  5. Squeeze, follow through, and watch how she reacts and where she goes.
  6. Turrets back to zero before you leave the gun, unless a second deer is standing there. Then lock the dial above and use the second-deer box.

Rifle & zero

Scope height is center of bore to center of scope. Measure the gap from the bottom of the scope tube to the top of the bolt body, then add half the tube (0.59″ for 30 mm, 0.50″ for 1″) and about 0.35″ (half the bolt). A ¼″ error is worth about one click at 500. Velocity: each factory speed comes from that maker's test barrel (listed under Loads); the default moves it 25 fps for every inch your barrel is shorter or longer. A chronograph reading under Loads replaces that estimate.

Scope & reticle

Your RPR came with a Vortex Diamondback Tactical 6–24×50 FFP. Its EBR-2C MOA reticle has a hash every 2 MOA, and the numbers printed on it are MOA, not yards and not a hash count. First focal plane means the hashes are true at every magnification, so range and hold at whatever power lets you see the deer best. Reticle check: the first hash below center is unnumbered and the second reads 4; at 100 yd the first hash covers about 2.1″. Tall-target check: at 100 yd, dial UP 20 clicks and fire; the hole should land about 5.2″ above your zero group. Dial back down and confirm it returns.

Loads · your data overrides the book

Chrono MV is your measured average from this rifle (blank uses book minus barrel loss). 100-yd offset: if this load prints high or right of your zero at 100, enter the inches (negative = low or left) and the whole card shifts with it. That lets every load run off one zero.

Sources & limits

Fusion 150 — Federal F308FS1, 2820 fps, G1 .414, bonded. Federal lists 1,900 fps as the minimum for reliable expansion. Federal

Ballistic Silvertip 168 — Winchester SBST308A, 2670 fps, G1 .474, polymer tip, not bonded. Some tables list .490; only .474 reproduces Winchester's own downrange velocities, so .474 is used. Winchester

American Whitetail Tipped 150 SST — Hornady 80934, 2810 fps, G1 .415, polymer tip, not bonded. Hornady

.350 Legend 170 InterLock — Hornady 81196 (American Whitetail), 2200 fps from a 20″ test barrel, G1 .215. That BC reproduces Hornady's own downrange speeds (1843 / 1529 / 1271 fps at 100 / 200 / 300) within 1 fps. Hornady

.350 Legend 165 FTX — Hornady 81197 (American Whitetail Tipped), 2200 fps from a 24″ test barrel, G1 .250 as Hornady lists it; that reproduces 1890 / 1612 / 1371 fps within 1 fps. Neither .350 load has a published expansion floor, so the tool doesn't flag one. Hornady

.350 barrel — Ruger lists 22″ for the American Predator Gen II in .350 Legend, so that's the default. With the 25 fps/in estimate that puts InterLock at about 2250 fps and FTX at about 2150. A chronograph number beats both.

Crossfire II Straight-Wall BDC — second focal plane, marks true at 9×: 1st hash 2 MOA, 2nd 4.6, 3rd 7.8, top of the bottom post 11.4, per Vortex's reticle manual. No wind marks on the crossbar.

Engine: point-mass, standard G1 drag table, RK4. Checked against the makers' own trajectory tables at their standard atmospheres: within 0.15″ of path and 3 fps at every 100 yd to 500 for the three .308 loads, and within 1 fps to 300 for both .350 loads. That proves the math, not your rifle. Expansion minimums for the two tipped bullets are not published; 1,800 fps is an estimate. Wind is treated as steady the whole way, which it never is. Not modeled: uphill or downhill angle, Coriolis.
